## Our paper: On Test-Time Scaling for Vision-Language Models

Can test-time scaling methods developed for LLMs be directly applied to Large Vision-Language Models? In this work, **Fawaz Sammani**, **Tzoulio Chamiti**, and **Nikos Deligiannis** present the first comprehensive study of test-time scaling for LVLMs — spanning multiple models and sizes, nine test-time scaling methods, and six benchmarks.

Key findings:

- Small, well-performing models benefit the most from test-time scaling, with gains of up to **~30%**, often matching or outperforming much larger models,
- LVLMs **lose focus** when given more compute than necessary,
- Visual information is encoded **early** in the reasoning chain, after which text-only reasoning dominates and image tokens contribute much less.

📄 Paper: [arxiv.org/abs/2606.28864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28864)
