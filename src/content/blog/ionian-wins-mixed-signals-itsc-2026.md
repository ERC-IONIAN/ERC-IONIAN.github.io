🏆 The **IONIAN** team won **first place** in the **Mixed Signals: V2X Collaborative 3D Object Detection Challenge**, held at the **IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC 2026)**!

## The challenge

The competition is built on the [Mixed Signals](https://mixedsignalsdataset.cs.cornell.edu/) dataset (ICCV 2025), a diverse, real-world point cloud dataset for heterogeneous LiDAR V2X collaboration. Each scene is captured by three connected vehicles and a roadside unit with dual LiDAR, giving five LiDAR streams from different viewpoints.

The goal is to detect **vehicles**, **bikes**, and **pedestrians** in the bird's-eye-view plane. Because each sensor sees the scene from a different position, fusing their point clouds helps to fill in occluded areas and extend detection range far beyond what a single viewpoint can see. Teams were free to choose any fusion strategy (early, intermediate, or late), and entries were ranked by BEV average precision on a held-out test set.
