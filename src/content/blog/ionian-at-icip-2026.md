The **IONIAN** team is at the **2026 IEEE International Conference on Image Processing (ICIP)** in Tampere, Finland, with **3 publications**! 🎉

- 🔦 **1 journal paper** presented as a **spotlight** in a special session
- 🎤 **1 oral** presentation
- 📄 **1 main conference paper**

![The IONIAN team at ICIP 2026](/images/group_icip.jpeg)

## Spotlight — Brent De Weerdt

**Brent De Weerdt** presented our IEEE Open Journal of Signal Processing paper, **"Neural Rate-Adaptive LDPC Decoding for the Slepian-Wolf Problem"**, as a spotlight in the special session on **Task-Oriented Source Coding**.

🔗 [Session details](https://event.icip2026.exordo.com/session/110/special-session-task-oriented-source-coding-3) · 📝 [Read more on the blog](/blog/neural-ldpc-slepian-wolf/)

<img src="/images/Brent_ICIP.jpeg" alt="Brent De Weerdt presenting at ICIP 2026" style="width:100%;max-width:420px;display:block;margin:1rem auto;" />

## Oral — Alexandre Stenger

**Alexandre Stenger** gave an oral presentation in the **Segmentation, Object Detection and Vision** session.

🔗 [Session details](https://event.icip2026.exordo.com/session/96/segmentation-object-detection-and-vision-1)

## Main conference — Ngoc Tan Le

**Ngoc Tan Le** presented **"GATA2Floor: Graph Attention for Floor Counting in Street-View Facades"**, a multi-head GATv2 architecture that models each facade as a vertical-aware graph to jointly count floors and assign windows to floors.

📄 [arxiv.org/abs/2605.11863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11863) · 📝 [Read more on the blog](/blog/gata2floor-icip-2026/)
